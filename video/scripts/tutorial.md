# How to play — 80-second tutorial

Voice: warm storyteller, unhurried, a little wry. One sentence per shot. Captions on.

| # | Shot (from the capture harness) | Narration | ~s |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Title card, then the accident montage (goose, scaffold, trebuchet cards fanned) | *In a medieval village, everyone smiles across the market square. And everyone keeps having accidents.* | 7 |
| 2 | Envelope opens: a role card flips (Jeweler) | *You run a secret trade. Nobody knows which — and keeping it that way is the whole game.* | 6 |
| 3 | Gold board fills, one track climbing | *Gold is earned by trades, not by players. Every coin you bank is also a clue about who you are.* | 7 |
| 4 | Hand of cards, hover a wares card, then a mishap | *Your hand: wares that earn gold, and a few accidents — bees, geese, a falling roof tile.* | 7 |

| 6 | Piles shuffle and flip, coins fly to the board | *Every pile is shuffled before it's revealed. Nobody can prove who arranged the goose.* | 7 |
| 7 | A wound lands; the heal card next to it | *Attacks wound. Heals mend. Protect voids a whole pile. And every wound you still carry at the end costs a coin.* | 8 |
